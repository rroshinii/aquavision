How it works

Four steps from raw footage to measurable results.

  1. Upload / Capture

    Drop in an underwater image or video, or switch on a camera.

  2. Enhance

    Optional colour-cast correction, contrast boost and denoising to fight turbidity and low light.

  3. Detect

    The YOLO model finds objects and draws labelled boxes with confidence scores.

  4. Analyze

    Review counts, confidence and history, then download the annotated result.
